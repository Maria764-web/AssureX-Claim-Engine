The Workflow

How AssureX Works

Four intelligent steps from device registration to instant claim decision.

01

Register Device

Add your electronics with serial number and purchase invoice. AssureX auto-generates your manufacturer warranty certificate and tracks coverage dynamically.

02

Submit a Claim

Describe the hardware fault, upload receipt and damage photos. OCR processes all documents instantly — no manual data entry required.

03

AI Engine Analyzes

Two independent AI models examine damage evidence. OCR verifies all document data. Manufacturer policy exclusions checked automatically in milliseconds.

04

Instant Decision

Valid claims approved for Service Centre repair in under 60 seconds. Complex cases escalate to human Claim Reviewers with immutable audit trail.
