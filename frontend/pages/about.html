THE PROBLEM

Why Warranty Claims Need a Smarter Process

Electronics warranty management historically struggles with friction, delays, and incomplete information. AssureX solves the core issues that slow down customer service and warranty operations.

Too Much Manual Work

Manual document checking and paper-based verification slow down turnaround times and create operational bottlenecks for service teams.

Missing Evidence

Incomplete purchase receipts, blurred serial numbers, and absent photo evidence leave support teams guessing and claims stalled.

Warranty Uncertainty

Calculating active coverage windows, warranty tiers, and policy expiration rules across varied electronics models is complex and error-prone.

Inconsistent Decisions

Without centralized validation rules, different support representatives evaluate similar defects inconsistently, harming customer trust.

Duplicate Claims

Repeated or overlapping claim submissions across different channels consume unnecessary labor and increase the risk of duplicate payouts.

Long Review Cycles

Disconnected communication between customers, service centres, and adjudicators stretches resolution times from hours to several weeks.

OUR APPROACH

One Claim. Multiple Layers of Validation.

AssureX does not rely on a single signal. Every warranty submission is evaluated across six structured checkpoints to ensure accuracy, policy compliance, and auditability.

01

Document Intelligence

OCR processes purchase receipts, invoices, and serial numbers, extracting structured metadata directly from uploaded files.

02

Data Validation

Cross-checks device serial numbers against registration records and checks for prior duplicate submissions across the database.

03

Python AI Analysis

A Python-based classification model evaluates structured claim metrics and defect patterns, generating a prediction with a confidence score.

04

Visual AI Analysis

A Google Teachable Machine model inspects the generated Claim Summary Card image as an independent, secondary visual opinion.

05

Warranty Rules

Deterministic policy checks verify warranty activation dates, expiration limits, exclusions, and prior repair histories.

06

Final Decision

System synthesizes AI predictions with warranty rules for an automated outcome or routes edge cases to human review.

TRUST & SAFETY

When AI Is Uncertain, Humans Stay in Control.

AssureX is designed with a human review safety net. When evidence is incomplete, models disagree, confidence is low, or warranty rules identify a contradiction, the claim can be routed for human review.

Low Confidence

When structured prediction confidence falls below the strict validation threshold.

AI Disagreement

When the structured Python model and visual Teachable Machine deliver conflicting assessments.

Missing Evidence

When uploaded invoices are obscured, serial tags are unreadable, or proof documents are absent.

Rule Conflict

When user fault descriptions contradict policy coverage limits or warranty exclusion terms.

Manual Review Ready

Uncertain submissions are securely queued for authorized Claim Reviewers to examine evidence and make deliberate determinations.
